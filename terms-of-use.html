Bayda is a tracking tool. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Calorie, macro and nutrition figures, calculated targets and estimates of energy burned are estimates, and so are the figures from the AI food scan, which reads a photo of a meal and guesses what is on the plate and in what amount. They can be wrong, sometimes by a lot. Check them before you rely on them.

Talk to a professional before you change your diet or start or increase exercise, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have had an eating disorder. Stop and seek help if you feel unwell. You use Bayda and its numbers at your own risk.
